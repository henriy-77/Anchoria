Risk Disclosure & Terms and Conditions

For Diaspora Investors Without a CSCS Account (Non-NGN KYC / Omnibus Structure)

By subscribing under this structure, you acknowledge and agree to the following:

  1. Structure & eligibility. This offer is not marketed directly to international investors. Anchoria has structured a facilitation route — via Anchoria Securities Limited's (ASL) stockbroking infrastructure and Anchoria Asset Management's (AAM) fund management — for investors who lack direct access to the Nigerian market or do not qualify for the offering directly.
  2. Ownership of shares. Because you do not hold the Nigerian credentials required to open a CSCS account, your shares cannot be registered in your name at subscription. They are held on your behalf within ASL's nominee/omnibus structure, with a defined allocation recorded against your investment.
  3. Future transfer. If you later obtain the required Nigerian credentials (e.g. a BVN) and an individual CSCS account is opened for you, your shares will be transferred to you via a share transfer, not a secondary-market trade. Any cost associated with this transfer shall be borne by the investor.
  4. Nominee & custody. ASL acts as nominee and maintains the definitive record of your allocation and transaction history. AAM manages the underlying fund and sees ASL as its sole subscriber, with no direct visibility into individual investors.
  5. Fees. A management fee of 0.5% applies, billed periodically on the valuation of the fund.
  6. No secondary market rights. Your shares cannot be traded on the open market while held within this structure. Legal transfer to you individually occurs only through the process described above, subject to your eligibility and ASL's verification requirements.
  7. Investment risk. Your investment carries the standard risks of investing in Nigerian equities, including price volatility, currency/foreign exchange risk on funds remitted into Naira, and regulatory or political risk. There is no guarantee of allocation, pricing, or return.
  8. Regulatory status. ASL is licensed by the Nigerian Exchange (NGX) and regulated by the Nigerian SEC. This structure is not a public offer or solicitation in any jurisdiction where such an offer would be unlawful. You are responsible for confirming your eligibility to participate under the laws of your jurisdiction.

By signing or submitting this form, you confirm that you have read, understood, and accepted this disclosure, and that you are participating voluntarily and with full understanding of the nominee/omnibus arrangement described above.
